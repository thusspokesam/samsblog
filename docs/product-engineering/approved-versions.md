## Release approval

1. Identify the complete platform combination and proposed change.
2. Validate compatibility and run the approved validation, benchmark, and stability tests.
3. Record results, limitations, rollback method, and support requirements.
4. Obtain engineering approval before updating the approved baseline.
5. Communicate the effective date and any retirement or upgrade requirements.

## Exceptions

Record the affected scope, reason, risk, compensating controls, owner, expiry date, and approver in the controlled exception register.
