**Last reviewed:** Pending

## Entry requirements

- [ ] Deployment record complete.
- [ ] Validation accepted.
- [ ] Burn-in accepted.
- [ ] Exceptions documented and approved.
- [ ] Service scope and support responsibilities agreed.

## Operational handover

1. Assign the service owner, support team, engineering escalation, and coverage model.
2. Register assets, service dependencies, and lifecycle information in the approved system.
3. Confirm role-based access and approved credential storage.
4. Verify dashboards, logging, alert routing, and operational response.
5. Link approved deployment, recovery, incident, and maintenance runbooks.
6. Agree capacity ownership, provisioning controls, and customer-facing handover where applicable.
7. Record acceptance and any time-bound follow-up actions.

## Acceptance record

| Field | Required information |
| --- | --- |
| Service / deployment reference | Controlled record identifier |
| Engineering acceptance | Reviewer, date, and evidence |
| Operations acceptance | Owner, date, and evidence |
| Open actions | Owner, due date, and approved impact |
| Service activation | Authorized decision and effective date |

## After onboarding

Review early-life issues, confirm support readiness, and feed improvements back to Product Engineering and the deployment procedures.
